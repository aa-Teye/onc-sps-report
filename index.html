📖 Get Started Guide

Quick helper guidelines for ONC Shepherds

🙏 SPS Shepherd Guidelines

  • Conduct a brief 10-minute follow-up phone call with your assigned members weekly.
  • Ask about their well-being, share a quick spiritual encouragement, and pray with them.
  • Mark attendance (whether they were called or not) and submit report weekly.

⛪ Microchurch Shepherd Guidelines

  • Record attendance for physical microchurch meetings or online fellowship sessions.
  • Track topic coverage: indicate if the weekly Bible outline was fully covered.
  • Ensure all new souls won during meetings are captured with full contact and spiritual details.

📝 How to Submit a Report

  • Select your name, complete attendance checklist, log prayer requests/concerns, add any new souls won, review summary, and click Submit.
  • Reports are instantly saved and synced to the central administrative database.
