# CEO Diary - Multi-Business Empire & Life Operating System
## Executive Desktop, Web, and Mobile App for Managing 100 Businesses & Personal Life

---

## 🏢 Platform Overview

CEO Diary is an all-in-one executive operating system designed for CEOs, Founders, and Serial Entrepreneurs managing multiple business entities, high-level task delegations, personal finances, daily habits, and strategic decision logs in one unified environment.

---

## 🎯 Key Modules

1. **Multi-Company Financial Ledger (Up to 100 Businesses)**:
   - Tracks revenue, expenses, net profit, and cashflow per business entity.
   - Aggregate "Empire Net Worth" Dashboard.

2. **Executive Task Delegation Engine**:
   - Assign and monitor key tasks given to company directors, managers, and assistants across all business units.

3. **Daily Life & Habit OS**:
   - Executive morning/evening routines, high-priority daily task matrix, habit streaks.

4. **Strategic Decision Log & Encrypted Notes**:
   - Offline-first secure vault for executive board decisions, meeting minutes, and confidential ideas.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Architecture

- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript + Vite + TailwindCSS
- **Desktop Application**: Electron / Tauri (Windows & macOS)
- **Mobile Application**: React Native / Progressive Web App (PWA)
- **Database**: SQLite (Offline-First) + Neon Serverless PostgreSQL (Cloud Backup Sync)
